08 Safety checks and sanctions screening

ChainKit includes automated token safety checks and an address screening indicator. These use third-party data sources including GoPlus Labs, Honeypot.is, and an unofficial sanctions dataset.

  • A clean result is not a safety guarantee. A token contract can pass every automated check and still be part of a scam. New tokens are often absent from these databases entirely.
  • A flagged result is not proof of wrongdoing. Automated screening produces false positives. A sanctions indicator on ChainKit is a prompt to check the official register — it is not a legal determination, and I am not qualified to make one.
  • These features may be unavailable, out of date, or wrong at any time.

Do not use ChainKit as your compliance process. If a sanctions or legal question affects a real payment, consult a qualified professional and check the relevant government's official list directly.
