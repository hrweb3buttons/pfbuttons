ChainKit

Find a transaction when you don't know which chain it's on

—
Read-only by default. ChainKit will never ask for a seed phrase or private key, and only connects to a wallet if you choose to send a donation. · Lookups are sent to public blockchain APIs. Your history stays in this browser.

ChainKit is an independent, unofficial tool. It reads public blockchain data through third-party APIs and shows it back to you. Every tab except Deposits is general reference and is not affiliated with any exchange, wallet, or blockchain project. The Deposits tab lists the coins accepted by one specific site through its payment processor.

Information here is provided as-is for general reference. It is not financial, legal, tax, or compliance advice. Balances, prices, and safety checks can be incomplete, delayed, or wrong — always confirm anything important on the chain's own block explorer.

Price data from CoinGecko. Token safety data from GoPlus Labs and Honeypot.is. Sanctions screening is a best-effort check against an unofficial dataset and is never authoritative.
